Install

Two minutes, then it's always there.

Pick your system. The installer copies the panel into Adobe's extensions folder and allows After Effects to load it.

1

Download and extract

Download NothiToolkit_Windows.zip. In your Downloads folder, right-click it and choose Extract All…

The installer can't run from inside the zip, so this step matters.

NothiToolkit_Windows.zip
2

Double-click the installer

Open the extracted folder and double-click Install Nothi Toolkit. Windows may hide the “.bat” and list it as a Windows Batch File: that's the one.

If Windows shows “Windows protected your PC”, click More info, then Run anyway. It appears because the installer isn't signed yet.

3

Press any key

A black command window opens. That's the installer: don't close it and don't type anything in it.

When it says Press any key to continue, press any key (Enter, Space…). The installer finishes on its own and the window closes.

On a French Windows, the line reads “Appuyez sur une touche pour continuer”.

1

Download, unzip, open

Download NothiToolkit_Mac.zip and double-click it to unzip. In the folder, right-click Install Nothi Toolkit.command › Open, then confirm with Open.

A Terminal window runs for a second and says Done. You can close it.

NothiToolkit_Mac.zip
2

If macOS blocks it

Open System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll down to Security and click Open Anyway next to the installer's name. Then open it again.

Still stuck? Do it by hand: paste this in Terminal, then drag the com.nothi.toolkit folder into ~/Library/Application Support/Adobe/CEP/extensions/

for v in 9 10 11 12 13; do defaults write com.adobe.CSXS.$v PlayerDebugMode 1; done

Then, in After Effects

Same on Windows and Mac.

4

Open the panel

Restart After Effects, then go to Window › Extensions › Nothi Toolkit.

In some recent versions the menu is called Extensions (Legacy). Same thing.

5

Dock it and keep it

Drag the panel by its tab next to your other panels. Any size works: it switches to icons only in narrow spaces.

Then Window › Workspace › Save as New Workspace. The panel now opens with After Effects every time.

On first launch, a 3-step guide shows you around. You can reopen it from the ··· menu.

Guide

How each tool works.

Every screenshot below is the real panel. Hover any button in After Effects to get the same tips.

CreateCurvesBeatLayersStyleEffectsSearch & undo

Create

New layers without going through menus and dialogs.

  1. Select a layer in your timeline (optional).
  2. Click a tile: Text, Adjust, Solid, Null, Shape, Camera or Light.
  3. The new layer lands right above the selected one, with the same start and end.

Options (sliders icon)

Name
Give new layers a name instead of the default.
Color
Color used for solids and shapes.
Match selected layer
Same timing and position in the stack as the selection. Turn off to use the full comp.

TipA null is the quickest way to animate several layers together: create it, then parent the layers to it.

Curves

Speed curves you can apply in one click, and keep.

  1. Select two keyframes or more in the timeline.
  2. Click a curve: Easy Ease, Smooth, Silk, Expo Out, Expo In, Punch, Snap Out or Glide.
  3. To keep your own: select two keyframes with an ease you like, click Save. It appears as “My curve 1”.

Your curves

Rename
Double-click the tile.
Delete
Hover the tile and click the ×.
Use anywhere
Your curves also show up in the search.

Beat

Markers on the beat of your song, only on the part you actually use.

  1. Drag your audio layer from the timeline onto the drop zone. Or select it and click the zone.
  2. The panel listens to the part of the layer that's on the timeline (its trimmed in and out), shows the beats and the BPM.
  3. Move Sens. to the left for only the big hits, to the right for every beat.
  4. Click the waveform to listen from there and check the beats.
  5. Click Add markers. Done.

Put your clips on the beat

Snap
Each selected layer moves to start on the nearest beat.
Sequence
Selected layers play one after another, one per beat, each trimmed to the next beat.

Options (sliders icon)

Listen to
Full mix, or kick & bass only for heavy drops.
Markers on
The audio layer, or the composition.
Work area only
Limits the beats to your work area.
Replace, number
Clear previous markers first; number the markers.

TipUse WAV, MP3 or AIFF. If the audio sits inside a video file, export the audio first.

Layers

The layer chores, in one place.

  1. Anchor point: select layers, click one of the 9 points. The layer doesn't move on screen, even if it's scaled or rotated.
  2. Precompose: puts the selected layers in a new comp, trimmed to their length.
  3. Decompose: select a precomp layer to bring its layers back into the current comp, with their timing. The status bar tells you if something couldn't be kept (time remap, effects on the precomp…).
  4. Rename: renames layers by type and content, like “TXT – Title” or “VID – Clip 04”.

Options (sliders icon)

Trim to the layers
The precomp starts and ends with its layers.
Delete the precomp
After decomposing, only if it isn't used anywhere else.
Rename scope
Selected layers or the whole comp, with or without type tags.

Style

Like a format painter for layers.

  1. Select the layer whose look you like and click Copy. The panel lists what it copied.
  2. Select one or more layers and click Paste.

Options (sliders icon)

Include animation
Keyframes too, shifted to each layer's start.
Effects
Paste the effects, added to or replacing existing ones.
Blend
Blend mode, opacity and motion blur.
Scale & rotation
Off by default, so layers keep their framing.

Effects

Your go-to effects without digging through Effects & Presets.

  1. Select layers, click an effect in the list to apply it.
  2. Alt+click puts the effect on a new adjustment layer instead.
  3. Type in the search field to find any installed effect, plugins included. Click the star to add it to your favorites.

Search & undo

Every action of every tab, from the keyboard.

  1. Click on the panel once, then press Ctrl K.
  2. Type a few letters (“anc”, “glow”, “null”), pick with the arrows, press Enter.
  3. Your last 5 actions are always at the top.

Good to know

Change the shortcut
··· menu › Open the search, then press your keys.
Undo
After each action, an Undo button appears in the status bar. Ctrl/Cmd+Z works too.
Icon size
··· menu › Small, Medium or Large.
Status bar
Shows what just happened, or what's missing (“Select a precomp layer”).

TipAfter Effects keeps the keyboard for itself unless the panel is focused, so click the panel first.

FAQ

Questions.

Nothi Toolkit & Labs

What is Nothi Labs?

The free side of Nothi: tools for editors, made alongside the Nothi marketplace. Nothi Toolkit is the first one. New tools will show up on this site.

Is it really free?

Yes. No account, no trial, no watermark, no ads in the panel.

Which versions of After Effects work?

After Effects 2022 (version 22) and newer, on Windows and on macOS (Intel and Apple Silicon).

Why does Windows or macOS warn me about the installer?

The installer isn't signed yet, and both systems warn about unsigned scripts from the internet. It does two things only: it copies the com.nothi.toolkit folder into Adobe's extensions folder, and it tells After Effects to accept extensions that aren't signed. Open it in a text editor to read it, it's a few lines.

I don't see it in Window › Extensions.

Quit After Effects completely and open it again. If it's still missing, run the installer once more, then check that this folder exists:

Windows: %APPDATA%\Adobe\CEP\extensions\com.nothi.toolkit
Mac: ~/Library/Application Support/Adobe/CEP/extensions/com.nothi.toolkit

In some versions the menu is named Extensions (Legacy).

The search shortcut does nothing.

After Effects only sends keys to a panel that has focus. Click anywhere on the panel, then press the shortcut. You can also click the magnifier icon.

Beat can't read my audio.

Use a WAV, MP3 or AIFF layer. Audio inside a video file (MP4, MOV) may not be readable by the panel: export the audio, import it, and drop that layer instead.

Can it break my project?

Every action is a single undo step, and an Undo button appears after each one. Saving your project before big changes is still a good habit.

Does it collect any data?

No. Your settings (favorites, curves, shortcut) stay on your computer. The only thing the panel does online is check this site for a newer version. Details on the Legal page.

How can I contact you?

Write to nothicontact@gmail.com for a bug, an idea for a new tool, or anything else.

How do I update to a new version?

When a new version is out, the panel tells you: an Update button appears at the bottom of the panel, and in the ··· menu.

Download the new zip from this page, close After Effects, and run the installer again. It replaces the old version by itself, no need to uninstall first. Your favorites, curves and shortcut are normally kept.

The icons are too small (or too big).

Open the ··· menu and pick an Icon size: Small, Medium or Large. The panel remembers it.

How do I uninstall it?

Run Uninstall.bat (Windows) or Uninstall.command (Mac) from the zip, or delete the com.nothi.toolkit folder listed above.
