Install

Two minutes, then it's always there.

Pick your system. The installer copies the panel into Adobe's extensions folder and allows After Effects to load it.

1

Download and extract

Click download and pick your After Effects version: you get NothiToolkit_Windows.zip (2022 and newer) or NothiToolkit_Windows_Legacy.zip (2019 to 2021). In your Downloads folder, right-click it and choose Extract All…

The installer can't run from inside the zip, so this step matters.

Download for Windows
2

Double-click the installer

Open the extracted folder and double-click Install Nothi Toolkit. Windows may hide the “.bat” and list it as a Windows Batch File: that's the one.

If Windows shows “Windows protected your PC”, click More info, then Run anyway. It appears because the installer isn't signed yet.

3

Press any key

A black command window opens. That's the installer: don't close it and don't type anything in it.

When it says Press any key to continue, press any key (Enter, Space…). The installer finishes on its own and the window closes.

On a French Windows, the line reads “Appuyez sur une touche pour continuer”.

1

Download, unzip, open

Click download and pick your After Effects version, then double-click the zip to unzip it. In the folder, right-click Install Nothi Toolkit.command › Open, then confirm with Open.

A Terminal window runs for a second and says Done. You can close it.

Download for macOS
2

If macOS blocks it

Open System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll down to Security and click Open Anyway next to the installer's name. Then open it again.

Still stuck? Do it by hand: paste this in Terminal, then drag the com.nothi.toolkit folder into ~/Library/Application Support/Adobe/CEP/extensions/

for v in 9 10 11 12 13; do defaults write com.adobe.CSXS.$v PlayerDebugMode 1; done

Then, in After Effects

Same on Windows and Mac.

4

Open the panel

Restart After Effects, then go to Window › Extensions › Nothi Toolkit.

In some recent versions the menu is called Extensions (Legacy). Same thing.

5

Dock it and keep it

Drag the panel by its tab next to your other panels. Any size works: it switches to icons only in narrow spaces.

Then Window › Workspace › Save as New Workspace. The panel now opens with After Effects every time.

On first launch, a 3-step guide shows you around. You can reopen it from the ··· menu.

Guide

How each tool works.

Every screenshot below is the real panel. Hover any button in After Effects to get the same tips.

CreateLayersCopy styleCurvesBeatCaptionsPresetsSearch & undo

Create

In a tall window, the first tab holds everything you do with layers: new layers at the top, then the Layers tools, then Copy style. In a small window, Layers and Copy style get their own tabs again (after Curves and Beat), and the buttons grow to fill the panel.

  1. Select a layer in your timeline (optional).
  2. Click a tile: Text, Adjust, Null, Shape, Camera or Light. The new layer lands right above the selected one, with the same start and end.
  3. Paste image: copy an image anywhere (on Google: right-click › Copy image), then click the tile. It lands in your comp at the playhead, transparency kept, and is saved in Documents › Nothi Labs. A copied image link works too. Ctrl/Cmd+V on the panel does the same.
  4. Solid opens a color picker first (black by default): pick a swatch, the rainbow square for any color, or type a hex code. Then Create solid, or double-click a swatch. The little dot on the tile shows your last color.

Options (sliders icon)

Name
Give new layers a name instead of the default.
Match selected layer
Same timing and position in the stack as the selection. Turn off to use the full comp.

TipA null is the quickest way to animate several layers together: create it, then parent the layers to it.

Layers

Under the new layers in a tall window, or in its own tab in a small one.

  1. Anchor point: select layers, click one of the 9 points. The layer doesn't move on screen, even if it's scaled or rotated.
  2. Precompose: puts the selected layers in a new comp, trimmed to their length. The precomp layer gets the usual composition color, so you can tell it apart.
  3. Decompose: select a precomp layer to bring its layers back into the current comp, with their timing. The status bar tells you if something couldn't be kept (time remap, effects on the precomp…).
  4. Duplicate: select a precomp layer and click. You get a real copy of the precomp: go inside it, change anything, the original doesn’t move. In After Effects, a plain Ctrl+D keeps both layers linked to the same comp.
  5. Sequence: select all your clips and click. They play one after another, no gap. The bottom layer comes first (on the left) and the top layer last (on the right), like a stack you read from the bottom.
  6. Invert: flips the order of the selected clips: the first becomes the last. Click again to flip back.
  7. Rename: renames layers by type and content, like “TXT – Title” or “VID – Clip 04”.

Options (sliders icon)

Duplicate
As a new layer above (default), or switch the selected layer itself to the copy. “Copy nested precomps too” makes precomps inside the copy independent as well.
Sequence order
Bottom layer first (default), top layer first, or the order you clicked the clips.
Sequence start
Where the first clip is, or at the playhead.
Gap
Frames between clips. A negative number overlaps them, handy for crossfades.
Trim to the layers
The precomp starts and ends with its layers.
Delete the precomp
After decomposing, only if it isn't used anywhere else.
Rename scope
Selected layers or the whole comp, with or without type tags.

Copy style

At the bottom of the Create tab, or in its own tab in a small window. Like a format painter for layers, animation included.

  1. Select the clip whose look you like and click Copy. The panel shows its effects and how many keyframes they have.
  2. Pick how the animation is pasted with the Animation switch.
  3. Select one or more clips and click Paste.

Animation

Fit to clip
The keyframes stretch or squeeze to each clip’s length. A glow that ramps up over a 2 s clip ramps up over a 5 s clip too, and the speed graph keeps exactly the same shape: same eases, same curves.
Keep
Same timing as the original, starting at each clip’s start.
Off
Effects only, with their current values.

Options (sliders icon)

Effects
Paste the effects, added to or replacing existing ones.
Blend
Blend mode, opacity and motion blur.
Scale & rotation
Off by default, so layers keep their framing.

Curves

Draw your ease on a graph, apply it, keep it.

  1. Select two keyframes or more in the timeline.
  2. Drag the two white handles in the graph. No need to hit them exactly: grab anywhere near a handle. The green dot shows how the motion will feel.
  3. Click Apply. Or pick a curve from the library below: it loads in the graph and applies right away.
  4. Like it? Click +, give it a name, press Enter. It joins your library.

In the graph

Shift + drag
Keeps the handle flat, like a classic ease.
Scroll
Zooms in and out where your mouse is, to place a handle precisely. Hold Ctrl (Cmd on Mac) to stretch only the width, Shift to stretch only the height. The − + buttons zoom both.
Drag the empty space
Moves around the graph when you're zoomed in.
Double-click
Fits the whole curve back in view (or the frame button).
Linear
Is now a curve in the library, first in the list.
Value / Speed
Value graph by default. Switch to Speed to work like After Effects’ speed graph: drag a handle sideways for the influence, up or down for the speed.
Invert
The ⇄ button flips the value graph upside down, for a value that goes down (an opacity fading out, a layer moving left): the high point moves to the left. The ease stays the same.
The numbers
Click them to type exact values, like 0.33, 0, 0.67, 1.
Read
The copy icon loads the ease of the selected keyframes into the graph, to tweak it.
Apply while editing
In the options: applies each time you release a handle.

Your curves

Rename
Double-click the tile.
Delete
Click the × on any tile, built-in curves included. Options › “Bring back the built-in curves” restores them; “Delete all my curves” clears your library.
Use anywhere
Your curves also show up in the search.

Beat

Markers on the hits of your song, only on the part you actually use. Beat listens to the bass (kick and bass line) and keeps its highest peaks: the moments you want your cuts on.

  1. Open the Beat tab and select a layer with sound in the timeline: it loads by itself. A song, a WAV, or a video (MP4, MOV): for videos, After Effects extracts the sound first, which takes a few seconds the first time.
  2. The waveform shows only the part that's on your timeline (between the layer's in and out points), with the beats and the BPM. Trim the layer and it updates.
  3. Move Sens. to the left for only the biggest hits, to the right to also get the smaller bass notes.
  4. Click the waveform to listen from there and check the beats.
  5. Click Add markers. Done.
  6. To do another song, click the ✕ on the waveform, then select the other layer.

Put your clips on the beat

Snap
Each selected layer moves to start on the nearest beat.
Sequence
Selected layers play one after another, one per beat, each trimmed to the next beat.
Cut
Splits the selected clips at every beat, like the razor tool: each piece becomes its own layer, ready for a different effect on each hit.

Options (sliders icon)

Markers on
The audio layer, or the composition.
Work area only
Limits the beats to your work area.
Replace, number
Clear previous markers first; number the markers.

TipWAV, MP3 and AIFF load instantly. If a video’s sound can’t be read, export it as WAV and use that layer.

Captions

Speech to text layers, timed to the voice. It runs on your computer: your audio is never uploaded.

  1. Select the clip with the voice in your timeline (video or audio, any length). Trim it to caption only a part.
  2. Pick Phrases (a few words at a time) or Word by word (one word at a time, TikTok style). For phrases, set how many words fit on screen.
  3. Click Auto captions. The first time, the panel downloads its speech model (142 MB, only once). Then it listens and creates one text layer per caption, each starting and ending with the words.
  4. Captions follow the sentences: a short question like “Are you all right?” stays in one piece, and longer sentences are split evenly, after a comma when there is one.
  5. Fix a word? Double-click the text layer in the comp, like any text.

Your own look

Caption style
Style a text layer the way you like (font, size, color, effects, even an animation) and name it Caption style. Every caption copies it, and its animation plays at the start of each caption.
Without it
White text with a thin black outline, centered at the bottom.

Split text

Words, Lines, Letters
Select text layers and click Split: each word (or line, or letter) gets its own layer, exactly where it was. The original layer is turned off, not deleted.
Anchor
Each piece gets its anchor point in its center, ready to scale or rotate (can be turned off in the options).

Edit text layers

Select all text
Selects every text layer of the comp in one click.
X, Y, Scale
Move or resize all the selected layers at once: drag the letter like in After Effects (Shift: faster) or type a value. Animated layers move by the same amount, so their animation stays.
Staircase
New captions go right above the clip, the first one lowest and each next one above it: a staircase going up in the timeline. Invert order flips the selected layers into a staircase going down (or back). The options can make “going down” the default.

Options (sliders icon)

Accuracy
Fast (142 MB) for clear voices, Best (466 MB) for accents, music under the voice or noisy clips. Slower.
Language
“Detect” works for most videos. Pick the language if words come out wrong.
Position, UPPERCASE, punctuation
Bottom or center, all caps, with or without commas and periods.

TipSpeed depends on your computer: a short clip takes seconds, a long interview can take a few minutes. “Best” accuracy is slower than “Fast”.

Presets

Your own library: a look you built once, ready in one click on any project. It works like After Effects’ Save Animation Preset.

  1. In the timeline, select what to keep: one or more effects, a text animator, masks, shape groups, or properties like Position or Scale with their keyframes and expressions.
  2. Click Save, name it, press Enter.
  3. Later: select layers and click the preset. Effects and animators are added, properties are set, and the animation starts at the playhead.

In After Effects too

AE button
On each preset. It opens After Effects’ own Save Animation Preset window: the preset becomes a real .ffx file in your User Presets, in the Effects & Presets panel (☰ › Refresh List to see it).

Folders

New folder
The folder button next to Save. Click a folder to open it, “Presets” at the top to go back.
Drag and drop
Drag a preset onto a folder to move it in, onto “Presets” to move it out, or between two presets to change the order.
Rename, delete
Double-click a name to rename it, × to delete.

TipPresets are saved on your computer and stay when you update the panel. They also show up in the search.

Search & undo

Every action of every tab, from the keyboard.

  1. Click on the panel once, then press Ctrl K.
  2. Type a few letters (“anc”, “glow”, “null”), pick with the arrows, press Enter.
  3. Your last 5 actions are always at the top.

Good to know

Change the shortcut
··· menu › Open the search, then press your keys.
Undo
After each action, an Undo button appears in the status bar. Ctrl/Cmd+Z works too.
Icon size
··· menu › Small, Medium or Large.
Look
··· menu › Look: a theme in one click, or your own background, text and accent colors. Add an image, a GIF or a video (MP4 or WEBM, 300 MB max) behind the panel. Set its opacity and size, choose how it fits (Fill, Fit or Stretch) and whether the video loops. Long MP4 videos keep their first 20 seconds.
Status bar
Shows what just happened, or what's missing (“Select a precomp layer”).

TipAfter Effects keeps the keyboard for itself unless the panel is focused, so click the panel first.

What is Nothi Labs?

The free side of Nothi: tools for editors, made alongside the Nothi marketplace. Nothi Toolkit is the first one. New tools will show up on this site.

Is it really free?

Yes. No account, no trial, no watermark, no ads in the panel.

Which versions of After Effects work?

After Effects 2019 and newer, on Windows and on macOS (Intel and Apple Silicon).

When you click download, pick your version: 2022 or newer, or 2019, 2020 or 2021. Both have the same tools. Not sure which one you have? In After Effects, open Help › About After Effects (on Mac: After Effects › About After Effects).

Why does Windows or macOS warn me about the installer?

The installer isn't signed yet, and both systems warn about unsigned scripts from the internet. It does two things only: it copies the com.nothi.toolkit folder into Adobe's extensions folder, and it tells After Effects to accept extensions that aren't signed. Open it in a text editor to read it, it's a few lines.

I don't see it in Window › Extensions.

Quit After Effects completely and open it again. If it's still missing, run the installer once more, then check that this folder exists:

Windows: %APPDATA%\Adobe\CEP\extensions\com.nothi.toolkit
Mac: ~/Library/Application Support/Adobe/CEP/extensions/com.nothi.toolkit

In some versions the menu is named Extensions (Legacy).

The search shortcut does nothing.

After Effects only sends keys to a panel that has focus. Click anywhere on the panel, then press the shortcut. You can also click the magnifier icon.

Beat or Captions can't read my audio.

Audio files and videos (MP4, MOV, MKV…) all work, even a full movie: the panel reads only the part that’s on your timeline, in the background. If one file still fails, export its audio as WAV, import it, and select that layer instead.

Can it break my project?

Every action is a single undo step, and an Undo button appears after each one. Saving your project before big changes is still a good habit.

Does it collect any data?

No. Your settings (curves, colors, shortcut) stay on your computer. Auto captions run on your computer too: your audio is never uploaded. The panel only goes online to check this site for a newer version, and once to download the speech model the first time you use captions. Details on the Legal page.

Auto captions: what do I need?

Nothing to install. The first time you click Auto captions, the panel downloads its speech model (142 MB) and keeps it, so you need an internet connection once. After that it works offline.

It understands about 100 languages. If the words come out wrong, pick the language in the menu instead of “Detect”, or switch Accuracy to Best in the options.

How can I contact you?

Write to nothicontact@gmail.com for a bug, an idea for a new tool, or anything else.

How do I update to a new version?

When a new version is out, the panel tells you: an Update button appears at the bottom of the panel, and in the ··· menu.

Click it: the right zip for your system and your After Effects downloads by itself. Close After Effects, extract the zip and run the installer again. It replaces the old version, no need to uninstall first. Your curves and settings are kept.

The icons are too small (or too big).

Open the ··· menu and pick an Icon size: Small, Medium or Large. The panel remembers it.

How do I uninstall it?

Run Uninstall.bat (Windows) or Uninstall.command (Mac) from the zip, or delete the com.nothi.toolkit folder listed above.
